Provenance & Craftsmanship

Our Story & Sourcing Journey

From indigenous paddy fields to precision small-batch milling and airtight delivery in Nagpur.

01 • Origin & Cultivation

Cultivated in Authentic Heirloom Terroirs

Our grains are sourced directly from traditional agricultural clusters known for indigenous soil fertility and unhurried cultivation. Black Rice originates in nutrient-rich wetlands, while Balaghat Chinnor thrives in the Wainganga river basin celebrated for its sweet groundwater.

02 • Milling & Sorting

Gentle Small-Batch Milling & Purity Sorting

We employ temperature-monitored, small-batch milling that cleans and sorts without harsh friction. For Black Rice, the anthocyanin-rich deep purple bran layer remains completely intact with zero artificial additives.

03 • Packaging & Delivery

Food-Grade Packing & Timely Dispatch Across Nagpur

Every harvest is packed under strict hygienic conditions into multi-layered moisture-barrier pouches and wholesale sacks. From our central facility in Kamptee, Nagpur, we maintain dedicated supply schedules for hospitality banquets and households.
